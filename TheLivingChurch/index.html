Who We Are

Rooted in Jesus. Growing together.

The Living Church is a Christ-centered family in Boise's foothills. We believe church isn't a building or a Sunday routine — it's people, living out the good news of Jesus with each other and for our city.

We keep it simple: love God, love people, and take the hope of the gospel wherever we go — from our own neighborhoods to the other side of the world.
